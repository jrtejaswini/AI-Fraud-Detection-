    Why not just accuracy?

    In the ULB dataset only 492 of 284,807 transactions are fraud (0.17%). A model that says "genuine" every time scores 99.83% accuracy and catches zero fraud.

    So we track precision, recall, F1 and PR-AUC. Recall matters most: a missed fraud costs more than a flagged genuine payment.
